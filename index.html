Hanoi’s office buildings are paying to cool and filter space no one is using.

HVAC is the largest controllable share of a Hanoi office’s energy bill, yet it runs on a fixed schedule, blind to whether a floor is full or empty.

The building already owns the hardware. What’s missing is the intelligence to use it.

Electricity keeps getting pricier and climate targets stricter, yet most climate control still runs the same way whether a floor is empty or full.

We’re not replacing your HVAC. We’re giving it the intelligence to run itself.

Most “smart building” software stops at recommendations. AirAI closes the loop all the way to the equipment.

The actuation layer reaches buildings competitors can’t.

Software-only competitors assume electronic actuators are already in place. Most Hanoi offices still have mechanical, pneumatic or manual valves and dampers, so AirAI Full installs them.

The overlay model is already proven abroad. Comparable systems have delivered:

  • 15 to 25% cost reductions
  • 20 to 40% carbon reductions

Sources: industry case studies (2020, 2021).
